100 years of journalism. Available across AI apps.

Haaretz wanted to make its extensive archive available to AI applications while maintaining control over subscriptions, access and editorial standards.

Raily connected the existing content management and authentication systems, enabling AI applications to access 180,000 articles spanning more than 100 years. The integration required under two hours of customer engineering time.
