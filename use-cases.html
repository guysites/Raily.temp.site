A new distribution channel
Your next audience may not be human.

AI agents are becoming a new channel through which people discover information, make decisions and interact with organizations.

For organizations with valuable data, content or expertise, this creates a significant opportunity: make that value available wherever people use AI.

Getting there requires more than publishing an API. Your information must be understandable to AI agents, accessible through the right interfaces, and governed by your existing business rules.

Raily makes that possible.

Who Raily is for

Make your knowledge accessible to the AI ecosystem.

03 / Enterprises & business ecosystems

Bring your business into agent interactions.

Make relevant organizational information and services accessible to customers, partners and suppliers through their AI systems.

  • Enterprise service providers
  • Partner ecosystems
  • Digital customer channels
A shared foundation

One governed way to reach external AI.

Connect existing data, build context across sources, and expose approved information through interfaces that respect your organization's access rules.

Without replacing the systems that already work or building a separate integration for every AI ecosystem.
