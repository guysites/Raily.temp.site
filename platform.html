Beyond connectivity

Connecting an agent to your data isn't enough.

An API can expose information. An MCP endpoint can make tools available. But neither automatically makes your organization's knowledge accessible and useful to AI agents.

Real-world data is fragmented across systems and formats. Its meaning is often implicit, relationships are spread across sources, and access is governed by permissions and business rules. Raily provides the layer to prepare, contextualize and govern that access.

01 — CONNECT

Bring your data together.

Connect structured and unstructured information across databases, content systems, object storage, enterprise platforms and existing vector databases.

02 — CONTEXTUALIZE

Give AI agents more than raw data.

Connect relationships across sources, organize information into coherent context, and make schemas, terminology and meaning understandable.

03 — EXPOSE & CONTROL

Make data available on your terms.

Expose information through MCP and APIs, applying identity, permissions, business rules and content restrictions.

Fits your environment
